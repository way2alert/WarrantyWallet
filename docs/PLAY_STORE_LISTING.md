## 2. Full Description

**Never lose a purchase bill again. Never miss a free warranty service deadline.**

How many times have you faced a broken smartphone, washing machine, or laptop, only to search through cluttered drawers looking for a faded paper receipt? Or realized that your 2-year warranty expired just two weeks ago?

**Warranty Wallet** is your clean, private, and smart digital locker for all purchase receipts, bills, invoices, and warranties.

---

### 🛡️ Why Choose Warranty Wallet?

- ⚡ **Almost Zero Typing with AI OCR**: Don’t spend 10 minutes typing out product fields. Snap a quick photo of your paper receipt or upload a PDF invoice. Our on-device text scanner automatically detects the product name, merchant, purchase date, price, and warranty duration for you to review and confirm with one tap.
- 🔔 **Proactive Expiry Reminders**: Receive automated notifications 30 days, 7 days, and 1 day before your manufacturer warranty ends. Claim authorized repairs or replacements before it’s too late!
- 📶 **100% Offline-First**: Works seamlessly without any internet connection or mandatory account creation. All records are stored privately on your phone.
- ☁️ **Optional Secure Cloud Backup**: Sign in with Email or Google whenever you want to sync your warranties across multiple devices and safeguard receipts against lost or damaged phones.
- 📂 **Organized Bill & Document Vault**: Keep high-resolution receipt photos and retailer PDFs (Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Apple, etc.) organized by category.
- 📊 **Asset Protection Dashboard**: Track the total financial value of all your protected appliances and gadgets in one glance.
- 🔍 **Instant Global Search**: Find any bill in seconds by product name, brand, store, serial number, or model number.
- 📤 **Data Export & Privacy Rights**: Export your complete warranty records to CSV or JSON anytime. We never sell your personal purchase data or run third-party advertising trackers.
